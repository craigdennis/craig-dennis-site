# CASE STUDY

---

**Company:** WriteSaaS (personal project)
**Role:** Founder / Builder
**Scope:** Product development, SEO architecture, AI-assisted build, growth

---

## The Problem

Job boards are generic. A content marketer, SEO strategist, or product marketer searching for B2B SaaS roles has to wade through noise on LinkedIn and Indeed to find anything relevant.

I was doing exactly that — 300+ applications deep into my own job search — and there was no focused destination for this specific audience.

So I built one.

---

## What I Built

WriteSaaS is a niche job board for content, SEO, and product marketing professionals targeting B2B SaaS companies. Built from scratch using AI-assisted development (Cursor and Claude Code) and live within weeks.

The goal was to validate a real product idea using the same tools I advocate for with clients: SEO-led acquisition, AI-accelerated execution, and clean conversion infrastructure.

### Product Development

Built and shipped a fully functional SaaS product solo, using Cursor and Claude Code to accelerate development without sacrificing quality. From zero to live in a few weeks — no agency, no team, no technical co-founder.

### SEO Architecture

Designed the site's search architecture from day one to compound over time. Keyword strategy focused on high-intent queries in the content marketing and SEO hiring niche. Structured the site to build topical authority, not just rank individual pages.

### AI-Assisted Workflows

Used AI tooling across both build and growth — from code generation to content production — demonstrating in practice what I advise clients to do in principle.
